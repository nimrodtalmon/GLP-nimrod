Arbitration
Arbitration extends Organisation.
A *dispute* is between two participants of an organisation, and an *arbiter*, a role the bylaws name, rules on it. What a ruling does, a parameter of Arbitration, is said by a contract written from it.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga an organisation.
1. ■ Raise: Alice, a participant of Orga, may raise her dispute with Bob, a participant of Orga, to Carol, an arbiter of Orga.
2. ■ Rule: Carol may then rule on the dispute.
Logic pragmas
Arbitration extends Organisation.
A *dispute* is between two participants of an organisation, and an *arbiter*, a role the bylaws name, rules on it. What a ruling does, a parameter of Arbitration, is said by a contract written from it.
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons, Orga an organisation.
1. ■ Raise: Alice may raise her dispute with Bob in Orga to Carol, provided Alice is a participant of Orga, Bob is a participant of Orga and Carol is an arbiter of Orga.
2. ■ Rule: Carol may rule on the dispute of Alice and Bob in Orga.
Logic pragmas.
Vocabulary: w holds the dispute of x and y in z = dispute(w,x,y,z); w has ruled on the dispute of x and y in z = ruled(w,x,y,z).
x is an arbiter of z: x holds the role Arbiter in z and z has x in the role Arbiter.
After x raises x's dispute with y in z to w, which requires w does not hold the dispute of x and y in z: w holds the dispute of x and y in z.
After w rules on the dispute of x and y in z: no longer w holds the dispute of x and y in z and w has ruled on the dispute of x and y in z.
